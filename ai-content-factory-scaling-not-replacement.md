# Instagram AI info label: content policy for live and generated material

Type: content policy and decision table. Reader: a marketer, creator or developer building an AI-assisted content pipeline that publishes to Instagram and other Meta platforms. Goal: decide which content is made live and which may be generated, stay within Meta's labelling rules, and use AI where it scales work without replacing the person. Limits: summarises Meta's public labelling rules as of this writing and my own practice; check Meta's current help pages before relying on details

![AI-generated illustration of a content factory that scales instead of replacing](https://dxggowrfyirnanabowhr.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/site-images/site-ai-content-factory-scaling-not-replacement-1792627707.jpg)

## Meta's labelling rules (summary)

| Situation | Label behaviour |
|---|---|
| File carries industry-standard AI indicators written by the generator | Label applied |
| Creator discloses AI use when posting | Label applied |
| Content only edited with AI tools (since September 2024) | Label moved into the post menu |
| Content fully generated | Label stays visible |

Stripping indicators from files works against the platform rule; the account carries the risk. It also does not change audience response, which is driven by the material itself

## Observation behind the policy

In my own work, live Reels, photos and posts get many times more views and reactions than fully generated ones. A year of running a content factory showed me it scales content work, making it simpler and faster, but does not replace it

## Decision table

| Content type | Make live | Generation acceptable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Talking-head video about your work | Yes | No | The reason people follow |
| Behind the scenes, process, team | Yes | No | |
| Client results, reviews | Yes | No | Never fabricate |
| Diagram explaining a process | Optional | Yes | No expectation of a real photo |
| Article or post cover | Optional | Yes | |
| Abstract illustration | Optional | Yes | |
| Subtitles, captions, platform versions | n/a | AI does it | Text around live content |

## Division of work in my pipeline

| Task | Who |
|---|---|
| Topic discovery from 13 sources around the clock | AI |
| Fact check of names, numbers, dates and quotes before drafting | AI |
| Platform versions: Russian and English for Telegram and website, own versions for Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube | AI drafts, author approves each |
| Subtitles for live clips, about 2 minutes per clip after one word in Telegram | AI |
| Filming, choosing topics, final yes | Author |

Result: about an hour per post by hand became about 5 minutes to a ready preview; 84 posts published, none without approval. Setup including platforms takes about a week

## Measurement

Run this before changing strategy:

1. Export the last twenty or thirty posts per format
2. Flag each as live or fully generated (retouched photos count as live)
3. Compare average views, reactions and production minutes per group
4. Repeat monthly; look at trends, not single posts

## Implementation notes

Record the generated flag in the content plan at publishing time, not afterwards. Deciding later which posts were generated is unreliable, and the comparison above depends on it

Keep pictures replaceable. In my previews there is a button to swap a generated image for the author's own photo or screenshot in one tap; real photos usually win

Write picture rules down: no other people's photos without rights, no generated images of real people. These are easier to agree on before the first complaint than after

Prefer live footage with AI around it. A clip filmed on a phone, with AI subtitles, cover and platform texts, takes little more time than a generated one and does not carry a visible label. That is the default this policy pushes toward: the person in the frame, the routine handled by AI

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Removing AI indicators from files | Breaks platform rules; audience response unchanged |
| A month of generated posts in one click | Feed looks like thousands of others; reactions drop |
| Generated content where people expect you | Loses the reason people follow |
| No human review before publishing | Positions you never took appear under your name |
