# My AI business assistant Jes

I developed an AI assistant for myself and call her Jes. She helps me every day

Her tasks include work, my website, creative projects and collecting ideas

She also helps with personal tasks, bookings and calendar meetings, and can receive inquiries and comments through my website

For example, I ask Jes to prepare posts for my website. She puts together the Russian text, an English version, a cover image and photos, and prepares versions for other platforms

Before publication, I receive the complete post for approval. I can ask for a rewrite or a different image, or cancel it. Until I approve publication, the post remains a draft
