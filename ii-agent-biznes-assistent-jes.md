# AI agents for business - how I work with Jes

![AI agents for business - how I work with Jes](https://dxggowrfyirnanabowhr.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/site-images/site-ii-agent-biznes-assistent-jes-1791298177.jpg)

I have an AI agent working in my business. I call her Jes. She is my business assistant, helping with tasks related to my work, website and creative projects

![Laptop with an AI assistant at work](https://images.pexels.com/photos/16094038/pexels-photo-16094038.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

## Preparing website posts

For example, I ask Jes to prepare posts for my website. She puts together the Russian text, an English version, a cover image and photos, and prepares versions for other platforms

Before publication, I receive the complete post for approval. I can ask for a rewrite or a different image, or cancel it. Until I approve publication, the post remains a draft

## Other tasks

I also turn to her for personal tasks. She helps me collect ideas, handle bookings and arrange meetings in my calendar

Jes can also receive inquiries and comments through my website
